3. DOWN PAYMENT VALIDATION
--------------------------------------------------

Currently a user can enter an unrealistically large down payment, such as a $10,000,000,000 down payment on a $32,000 vehicle.

Improve this behavior.

Do NOT show negative financing or negative loan payments.

If the entered down payment is equal to or greater than the amount that can actually be financed:

- Estimated amount financed should become $0.
- Monthly loan payment should become $0.
- Display a small, non-intrusive validation message such as:

"Down payment covers the financed amount. No financing required."

If the entered down payment exceeds the applicable purchase/financed amount, also communicate that the entered down payment exceeds the estimated amount due.

Do not silently produce confusing results.

Do not automatically replace what the user typed while they are actively editing the field. Preserve the entered value but safely cap the effective value used in financing calculations.

The calculator should never display a negative loan balance or negative monthly loan payment.

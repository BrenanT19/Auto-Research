STAGE 2B — PILOT VEHICLE #1: 2024 FORD MUSTANG ECOBOOST FASTBACK
Begin the first real-data vehicle pilot using the approved Stage 2B plan and completed Task #32 infrastructure.
Target candidate:
2024 Ford Mustang EcoBoost Fastback — U.S. market — 2.3L EcoBoost turbocharged I-4 — 10-speed automatic — RWD.
This is the first actual real-data pilot configuration. Work carefully and fail closed.
Phase 1 — Current-source and rights preflight
Re-fetch current official primary-source documentation rather than relying only on previously captured notes.
Prioritize:
- Ford official U.S. MY2024 technical specifications/order documentation and appropriate owner/service documentation.
- NHTSA official datasets/APIs for recalls, complaints/investigations, and NCAP only where the tested variant can be matched defensibly.
- EPA/FuelEconomy.gov only if the exact applicable vehicle/test record can be matched.
For every source actually used, record:
- publisher
- exact URL/dataset/API
- retrieval date
- document/dataset revision when available
- authority for the particular fact
- permitted access/use
- storage/retention status
- normalized/derived-value use
- public-display/redistribution status
- attribution requirements
- automation/rate restrictions
Do not infer permission merely because information is publicly accessible. If the intended use is not sufficiently established, mark it NEEDS RIGHTS VERIFICATION and do not ingest or publish material that depends on that unresolved permission.
Do not use Consumer Reports, paywalled automotive research, scraped proprietary databases, unofficial specification aggregators, or copied copyrighted prose as canonical evidence.
Prefer storing normalized factual values plus source metadata/evidence locators. Do not persist large raw copyrighted documents or copied source prose unless the applicable rights explicitly permit it.
Phase 2 — Exact configuration match
Complete a reviewer-readable match sheet before creating the canonical configuration.
Positively verify:
- United States market
- 2024 model year
- Ford consumer brand
- Mustang model
- correct generation
- EcoBoost trim
- Fastback body
- 2.3L EcoBoost engine
- 10-speed automatic transmission
- rear-wheel drive
- any material package/build-date predicates affecting the facts being loaded
Do not create the configuration from independent option lists or Cartesian combinations. If this exact combination cannot be established from dated primary evidence, stop and report the mismatch rather than guessing or broadening the identity.
Phase 3 — Create the first real canonical draft
Only after the source-rights and exact-match gates pass, create the canonical identity/configuration in the development environment using the Stage 2A/Task #32 architecture.
This must be a separate real canonical profile. Do not convert, overwrite, redirect, or attach real evidence to any of the existing 13 demo profiles.
Load only facts that are individually supported, properly scoped, permitted for the intended use, and reviewable. Missing information must remain unknown, not_yet_researched, or not_applicable as appropriate.
Do not fabricate completeness.
Do not require or invent:
- reliability score
- ownership-cost estimate
- lifespan estimate
- maintenance data
- issue frequency
- repair cost
- safety rating
- recall status
- inspection advice
unless that specific information passes its own evidence, applicability, rights, and review gates.
NHTSA complaints are research signals, not confirmed defects or prevalence statistics. Model-level recall matching must not be represented as VIN-specific open-recall status. NCAP ratings must not be generalized beyond a defensibly matched tested variant.

Run the relevant Task #32 and Stage 2B regression tests.

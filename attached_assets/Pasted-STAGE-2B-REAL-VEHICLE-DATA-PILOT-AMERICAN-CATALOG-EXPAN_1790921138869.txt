PRODUCT GOAL

AutoResearch should eventually provide a broad research catalog of American-origin automotive brands and their U.S.-market vehicles, followed later by German, Japanese, Korean, British, Italian, and other global brands.

For this project, “American” means American-origin consumer automotive brands with vehicles offered in the U.S. market. Do NOT use assembly location as the definition of whether a brand is American.

Keep these concepts separate:
- consumer brand/origin
- corporate manufacturer/owner
- market where vehicle is offered
- assembly/manufacturing location

The long-term goal is not merely 13 hand-built vehicle pages. AutoResearch needs a scalable catalog covering model → generation → model year → trim → exact supported configuration, while avoiding fake combinations and duplicate records.

LEGAL / DATA-USAGE REQUIREMENT

AutoResearch must take a conservative, permission-aware approach to data.

Only recommend sources whose data can reasonably and lawfully be accessed and used for the proposed purpose. Research the current documentation/terms for every proposed source before recommending ingestion, storage, redistribution, caching, or automated access.

Prioritize:
1. U.S. government/public automotive datasets and APIs where permitted.
2. Manufacturer/OEM information where the applicable terms permit the intended use.
3. Properly licensed data providers if needed later.
4. AutoResearch’s own original research/synthesis.

Do not propose scraping paywalled/proprietary automotive research sites or copying proprietary ratings, reliability databases, reviews, or editorial content.

If storage, redistribution, automation, caching, or commercial-use rights are uncertain, mark the source as NEEDS RIGHTS VERIFICATION and do not design the implementation as if permission exists.

Do not fabricate citations, source capabilities, licensing rights, API limits, vehicle facts, owner reports, reliability information, recalls, ratings, or specifications.

DATA QUALITY PHILOSOPHY

AutoResearch is a guide, not a verdict.

Real information must be distinguishable from:
- demo content
- verified/source-backed facts
- researched/editorial synthesis
- estimates/calculations
- externally researched owner-reported patterns
- direct AutoResearch owner submissions

A fact being found does not automatically make it verified or publishable.

Design a practical review lifecycle such as:
researched/captured → source-backed → reviewed/verified → publishable/published

The exact states may be revised if the existing Stage 2A architecture already provides a better mechanism.

Missing information must remain Unknown / Not Yet Researched / Not Applicable as appropriate. Never invent values merely to make a report look complete.

Do not require a numeric reliability score. Reliability research should be evidence-based and explain uncertainty.

EXACT CONFIGURATION / APPLICABILITY

Use the canonical hierarchy established in Stage 2A:
Consumer Brand → Model → Generation → Model Year → Trim → Vehicle Configuration

Vehicle Configuration represents a documented real combination, not a Cartesian product of possible options.

Powertrain/component information may include reusable/versioned:
- engines
- transmissions
- electric motors
- batteries
- hybrid systems
- drivetrain
- body/cab/bed configurations
- other configuration-specific equipment when necessary

Research must be fail-closed.

An engine-specific issue must not appear on every trim.
A battery-specific issue must not appear on unrelated battery versions.
A safety rating must not silently apply to an untested variant.
A recall campaign must not be represented as proof that a particular VIN has an open recall.

Missing or unresolved applicability means UNKNOWN, not universal.

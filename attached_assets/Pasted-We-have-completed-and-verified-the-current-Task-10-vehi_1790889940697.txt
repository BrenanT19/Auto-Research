==================================================
PRODUCT GOAL
==================================================

AutoResearch is intended to become a scalable, source-backed automotive research platform.

The goal is to help someone understand a vehicle beyond its purchase price by combining:

- vehicle specifications
- generations/model years/trims/configurations
- powertrain information
- reliability research
- known/reported issues
- maintenance expectations
- recalls and safety information
- ownership costs
- vehicle-specific inspection considerations
- owner experiences
- comparisons
- ownership calculations
- transparent sourcing

AutoResearch should be a GUIDE, NOT A VERDICT.

It should help users investigate vehicles without pretending that subjective reliability research or incomplete evidence is universally definitive.

Accuracy, applicability, transparency, and sourcing are extremely important.

Never design the system around fabricated automotive information, fake citations, fake owner reports, unsupported reliability scores, or false precision.

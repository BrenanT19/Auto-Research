# Recalls

![vehicle alert icon](https://www.nhtsa.gov/sites/nhtsa.gov/files/styles/thumbnail/public/2022-09/vehicle-alert-icon.png)NHTSA’s Office of Defects Investigation administers the agency’s safety recall program. This includes providing monitoring and verification of recall notifications and remedy campaigns by manufacturers, as well as providing accurate and timely recall information, including metrics, to the public.

On NHTSA.gov, consumers can search for recalls by:

- [license plate](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/2401),
- [vehicle identification number](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/2401),
- [vehicle year, make and model](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/2401), and
- equipment type such as [car seats, tires or other equipment](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls).

Other quick links
[Takata air bag recall](https://www.nhtsa.gov/equipment/takata-recall-spotlight) [Roles in the recall process](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls#23921) [State DMV Grants to promote recall notification](https://www.nhtsa.gov/press-releases/nhtsa-announces-three-states-awarded-dmv-recall-grants)

- by [date and product type](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/33631),
- by [NHTSA ID](https://www.nhtsa.gov/?nhtsaId=), and
- through an [interactive dashboard](https://data.transportation.gov/dataset/NHTSA-Recalls-by-Manufacturer/mu99-t4jn) where you can filter recalls.

We also make other recall reports and data available:

# Investigations

### Defects Investigations

![investigation icon](https://www.nhtsa.gov/sites/nhtsa.gov/files/styles/thumbnail/public/2022-09/investigation-icon.png)NHTSA conducts investigations concerning the identification and correction of safety-related defects in vehicles and vehicle equipment. Information about potentially defective products can come from a variety of sources including manufacturers, consumers, law enforcement agencies, media and online sources. NHTSA looks at all available data to assess the relative frequency and potential severity of any possible safety defect using an established [risk-based process](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/102611). Complaint information submitted to NHTSA is continuously reviewed to identify potential safety issues that warrant follow up. NHTSA may also use resources, such as the agency’s [Vehicle Research and Test Center](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/32306) and Special Crash Investigations, to assist with defect investigations. If NHTSA determines a safety-related defect trend exists, the agency may open an investigation to determine if the defect presents an unreasonable risk to safety that may warrant a recall by the manufacturer.

There are several types of defect investigations that NHTSA conducts: Defect Petition, Preliminary Evaluation, Recall Query and Engineering Analysis. NHTSA works to complete a Defect Petition within four months, Preliminary Evaluation within eight months, Recall Query within eight months and Engineering Analysis within 18 months.

NHTSA makes investigation information searchable:

- by [date](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/33631#investigation) of when an investigation was opened or closed,
- by [NHTSA ID](https://www.nhtsa.gov/?nhtsaId=), and
- through an [interactive dashboard](https://data.transportation.gov/stories/s/NHTSA-Investigations-by-Manufacturer/auu6-iy49) where you can filter investigations.

Investigation information is also associated with vehicles and equipment in NHTSA's database and is displayed when a user searches a vehicle or equipment through NHTSA's [Recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/2401) or [Ratings](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/961) webpages.

Related info: [Office of Defects Investigation quarterly metrics](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/100141)

### Compliance Investigations

![check icon](https://www.nhtsa.gov/sites/nhtsa.gov/files/styles/thumbnail/public/2022-09/check-icon.png)NHTSA tests vehicles and equipment that are available for sale to consumers to ensure that they meet Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards. We do this by purchasing vehicles and equipment from dealers and retailers, then testing them. If an item fails to meet federal safety standards when tested, NHTSA notifies the manufacturer. Test failures or other evidence of not meeting standards may lead to a compliance investigation.

Vehicle and equipment test results are available on [our website](https://one.nhtsa.gov/cars/problems/comply/).

- Related info: [Office of Vehicle Safety Compliance quarterly metrics](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/100146)

### Vehicle Importation

![checklist icon](https://www.nhtsa.gov/sites/nhtsa.gov/files/styles/thumbnail/public/2022-09/checklist-icon.png)NHTSA works with Customs and Border Protection to monitor the importation of vehicles and equipment to prevent goods not meeting U.S. standards from entering the marketplace. The agency reviews requests to allow importation of non-U.S. market vehicles under certain exceptions. Also, the agency acts to ensure that vehicles that are modified to meet U.S. standards by registered importers meet requirements and do not have safety defects.

Quick links:

- [Overview of importing a vehicle](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/2386)
- [Guidance documents](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/32176#52996)
- [Enforcement actions](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/102756#enforcement-actions)

### Special Crash Investigations

![car check icon](https://www.nhtsa.gov/sites/nhtsa.gov/files/styles/thumbnail/public/2022-09/car-check-icon.png)NHTSA's National Center for Statistics and Analysis conducts Special Crash Investigation cases, which are intended to be a data set useful for examining special circumstances or outcomes in crashes from an engineering perspective. Special Crash Investigations allow NHTSA to quickly locate and examine unique real-world crashes anywhere in the country and perform comprehensive clinical investigations. The data collected are used by the automotive safety community to improve the performance and development of advanced safety systems.

NHTSA's special crash investigation cases are [searchable online](https://crashviewer.nhtsa.dot.gov/SCI/SearchIndex).

Related info: [Special Crash Investigations](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/32376)

# Complaints

![computer icon](https://www.nhtsa.gov/sites/nhtsa.gov/files/styles/thumbnail/public/2022-09/computer-icon.png)Consumers who experience a vehicle issue that could be a safety defect are encouraged to file a complaint with NHTSA. Complaints can be filed through [NHTSA.gov](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/102086) or by calling NHTSA's Vehicle Safety Hotline at [888-327-4236](tel:888-327-4236).

NHTSA makes its complaints searchable by keyword on NHTSA's [Recall page](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/2401#vehicle).

Investigation information is also associated with vehicles and equipment in NHTSA's database and is displayed when a user searches a vehicle or equipment through NHTSA's [Recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) or [Ratings](https://www.nhtsa.gov/ratings) webpages.

- Related info: NHTSA Consumer Vehicle Complaint Process: [Webpage](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/2401#7756) \| [PDF](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/98351)

# Frequently Asked Questions

## Recall FAQs

### **I saw a recall for my make and model but haven’t received a notification. Where can I look to find out if my vehicle is affected by a safety recall?**

Visit [NHTSA.gov/Recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/2401) and enter your vehicle identification number. You can also download NHTSA’s free [SaferCar app](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/101906) and add your vehicle and VIN. When SaferCar discovers a recall for the vehicle you entered, it will send you an alert on your mobile device.

### **How do I find out if my child’s car seat has been recalled?**

Visit [NHTSA.gov/Recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/2401#car-seats), select the “Car Seats” tab, and search your car seat brand name and model.

### **Where can I find a list of recalls by manufacturer?**

[NHTSA has an interactive recalls dashboard](https://datahub.transportation.gov/dataset/NHTSA-Recalls-by-Manufacturer/mu99-t4jn) where you can filter by manufacturer.

### **Where can I find a list of recalls by product type?**

Visit [NHTSA.gov/search-safety-issues](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/33631) to search recalls by product type.

### **How can I see if a recall repair was previously completed on my vehicle?**

You can search by VIN at [NHTSA.gov/Recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/2401). If there is an unrepaired recall for the VIN you entered, it will display the unrepaired recall(s). If you still have questions, please contact the manufacturer.

### **Do recalls expire?**

Once a vehicle has been recalled, that recall never expires.

### **What is NHTSA’s bulk VIN lookup tool?**

NHTSA allows users to periodically [search up to 100 VINs](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls#other-search-options) to see if any vehicle has an unrepaired recall. This feature is not intended for frequent or excessive use and has protections in place to guard against bots and abuse. If a user, or multiple users within an organization, use this tool too frequently, they may receive an error message due to excessive usage.

## Investigation FAQs

### **Where can I find investigations conducted by NHTSA?**

Review the Investigations section of this webpage or visit [NHTSA.gov/Recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/2401) and search for the vehicle, car seats, tires or equipment. In the search results, you’ll see a link to investigations. All publicly available information will be added to the investigation record on NHTSA.gov, including opening and closing resumes, information requests, correspondence with the manufacturer and other official documents, once they have been redacted for confidential business information and privacy concerns.

## Complaint FAQs

### **How many complaints does it take to open an investigation?**

There is no set number. NHTSA reviews every complaint, along with other data, to determine if a potential safety defect trend exists.

### **I believe my vehicle has a safety defect. Where can I file a complaint?**

On NHTSA.gov, you should select “ [Report a Safety Problem](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/102086)” to file a complaint online or contact our Vehicle Safety Hotline at [888-327-4236](tel:888-327-4236).

### **My dealership said my recall repair** **was not covered by my warranty. Can I file a complaint with NHTSA?**

Yes, you can file a complaint on [our website](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/102086) or contact our Vehicle Safety Hotline at [888-327-4236](tel:888-327-4236).

### **Will I be contacted about my complaint submitted to NHTSA?**

If NHTSA experts require more information, receipts or photos, you may be contacted. Not every complainant is contacted.

### **Will NHTSA share my complaint with the manufacturer?**

When submitting a complaint, you will be asked if you want to share your complaint details, including VIN and identifying information, with the manufacturer.

### **How can I file a complaint on more than one vehicle at a time?**

Please contact our Vehicle Safety Hotline at [888-327-4236](tel:888-327-4236).

### **I believe my off-road vehicle has a safety defect. Can I submit a complaint to NHTSA?**

No, contact the [Consumer Product Safety Commission](https://www.cpsc.gov/) to file a complaint for off-road vehicles, such as ATVs, side-by-sides, off-road motorcycles, and go-carts.

## Data FAQs

### **How can I access and download data related to recalls, investigations, complaints or manufacturer communications?**

For data downloads, visit [NHTSA.gov/nhtsa-datasets-and-apis](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/102181).

### **How do I find Manufacturer Communications/Technical Service Bulletins?**

For more information on manufacturer communications for specific vehicles or equipment, visit [NHTSA.gov/Recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/2401) and search for the vehicle, car seats, tires or equipment. In the search results, you’ll see a link to manufacturer communication. Additional information is also available on our [vehicle manufacturer webpage](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/102986).

### **What Early Warning Reporting data are available to the public?**

Death and injury reports, light vehicle production numbers, property damage claims, manufacturer communications, and foreign recall reports are publicly available. For more information, visit [NHTSA.gov/vehicle-manufacturers/early-warning-reporting](https://www.nhtsa.gov/node/97516).

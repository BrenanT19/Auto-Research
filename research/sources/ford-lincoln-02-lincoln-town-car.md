# The Lincoln Town Car is no longer available.

## Produced from 1980 to 2011, the Lincoln Town Car defined automotive comfort and luxury for three decades. Though we no longer make the Town Car, its spirit lives on through generations of enthusiasts, and its impact can be seen in today’s lineup of Lincoln luxury SUVs.

### FIRST GENERATION 1981 - 1989

An evolution of the Lincoln Continental, the Town Car was released as its own line in 1981 and immediately became the Lincoln flagship model. Throughout the 80's, the Town Car introduced many advanced luxury features, like six-way power seats, a keypad-based keyless entry system, and with the 1985 update, the Lincoln Town Car was the first Ford vehicle to feature a CD player.
...less

![Shown here is a second generation Lincoln Town Car.](https://www.assets.ford.com/adobe/assets/urn:aaid:aem:bc78b6f5-30c6-4d88-9167-14c1ce4314c3/as/towncar-1991-3col-2.webp?max-quality=75&crop-names=1_3x2&width=480)

### SECOND GENERATION 1990 - 1997

Starting in 1990, the Lincoln Town Car body went through a major redesign, with a host of new conveniences. It was also the year it was named Motor Trend's Car of the Year, marking the first time a large luxury sedan took home the prize. It was an overwhelming sales success, instantly making the Town Car one of America's best selling luxury sedans, regularly exceeding 100,000 vehicles per year.
...less

![Shown here is a third generation Lincoln Town Car.](https://www.assets.ford.com/adobe/assets/urn:aaid:aem:23c17ecf-8451-4eed-8e00-fa3bdd71c46d/as/towncar-2000-3col-3.webp?max-quality=75&crop-names=1_3x2&width=480)

### THIRD GENERATION 1998 - 2011

For the 1998 model year, the Town Car enjoyed an extensive revision. The more boxy design of the ‘90s evolved into a curvaceous design scheme with a downward-sloping trunk lid, pursed lip grille and cat-eye headlamps. The third-generation Town Car was introduced at the same time as the Lincoln Navigator® SUV, the first Lincoln SUV and a nod to the future of Lincoln.

## FAQs

### Does Lincoln make the Town Car anymore?





Lincoln stopped producing the Town Car in 2011.


### Will Lincoln bring back the Town Car?





Although we have no immediate plans to bring back the Lincoln Town Car, we recognize its unique legacy and appreciate the continued interest in Town Car.


### Can I buy a certified pre-owned Lincoln Town Car?





No. We do not offer the Town Car, but have many Certified Pre-Owned Lincoln vehicles available for purchase.

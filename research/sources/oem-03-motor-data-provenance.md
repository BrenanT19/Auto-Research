# Our Data

## Delivering Value through Expertise and Precision

At MOTOR, we understand that accurate, reliable data is the backbone of the automotive industry. Our approach to data is rooted in a deep commitment to excellence, ensuring that every piece of information we deliver is accurate and actionable.

## Data Sourcing

Our journey begins with robust data sourcing. MOTOR secures parts and pricing information licensing agreements with each Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) to ensure the most comprehensive and current data. We also establish service information licensing agreements and industry taxonomy standards, ensuring that our data is extensive and standardized.

## Ingestion

Once sourced, our data goes through meticulous ingestion processes where we standardize formats and map vehicles, parts, and operations taxonomies. Our team’s detailed knowledge of each data source ensures the information is organized and ready for application in real-world scenarios.

## Editorial Expertise

Our editorial team brings unparalleled expertise in light-, medium- and heavy-duty mechanical, repair, labor estimating, and parts information. We also provide truck parts data, lubrication industry standards, aftermarket parts catalogs, and more. Our commitment to accuracy extends to graphics development and collision repair data, ensuring you receive the most reliable and comprehensive information.

## Industry Standards

MOTOR adheres to the highest industry standards, including ACES VCdb, ACES PCdb, VMRS and Chek-Chart, to deliver data that meets the most rigorous benchmarks for quality and usability.

## And More

Beyond sourcing, ingestion and adherence to industry standards, MOTOR offers extensive change management, project management, and business intelligence reporting services. Managing data is more than just collection and storage—it’s about transformation and insight.

## Output

Finally, MOTOR delivers data in the formats that best suit your needs. Whether you require bulk delivery by XML, CSV, or other formats, our web services, applications and white-label solutions are designed to seamlessly integrate into your systems, delivering the precise information you need when you need it.

## Why MOTOR?

With MOTOR, you’re not just getting data—you’re gaining a trusted partner who understands the complexities of the automotive industry and is dedicated to delivering value through data. Our approach is comprehensive, detail-oriented, and rooted in a deep understanding of both the industry and your business needs.

Normalized evidence (paraphrase): Tesla's release identifies the vehicle as the 2008 model-year Roadster, announces commencement of regular production, and says the 2008 model year had sold out while the company was accepting reservations for the 2009 model year. This is direct evidence for MY2008 and a 2009 model-year offering/reservation, but not by itself evidence of each delivery date, all U.S. sales, or every later year. The 2012 endpoint remains unresolved by the evidence consulted here.

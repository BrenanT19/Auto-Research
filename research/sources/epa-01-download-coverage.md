- Source: https://www.fueleconomy.gov/feg/download.shtml
- Title: Download Fuel Economy Data
- Retrieved: 2026-10-08. Page states its all-years downloadable dataset spans MY1984–MY2026; at review it lists a preliminary MY2027 guide/data file separately. It identifies EPA-supervised/manufacturer testing as the basis and notes retroactive MPG estimate adjustments for some years.
- What this establishes: published FuelEconomy.gov downloadable fuel-economy/vehicle data bounds and published history as of retrieval. The existence of a dataset for a year is not proof of exhaustive U.S. retail identity coverage.

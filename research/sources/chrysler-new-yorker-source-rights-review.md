# Chrysler New Yorker source access and reuse review

- Reviewed: 2026-10-08.
- Scope: source access and source-use conditions for the 35 selected New Yorker model-year identity checks, MY1940–1942 and MY1946–1977. No production data or release settings changed.

## Auto-Brochures

- The site's About page says older printed brochures are scanned and that the collection is mainly U.S.-market literature. The 1952 and 1956 brochures were inspected as factual sources only.
- The checked `robots.txt` and `_terms.html` paths returned the site's 404 page. No express automated-access prohibition or license for the site's hosted scans was found in the reviewed material.
- The absence of a located prohibition is not a permission grant. The archive's rights to host each scan, and any rights for commercial retention/derivation, attribution, or public display of facts, remain unresolved. No brochure prose, tables, or images were retained.
- Site overview: [Auto-Brochures About](https://www.auto-brochures.com/_about.html).

## The Old Car Manual Project

- The homepage says much material may be public domain, asks users not to copy material without permission, and describes the collection as historical/educational research material. These general statements are not item-specific proof of public-domain status or a reuse license.
- A folder title for a 1950 Chrysler Saratoga/New Yorker brochure was encountered, but the original scan's text was not verified. It remains a lead only and is not evidence in the profile matrix.
- No text or images from the 1950 folder were used. See the [collection homepage](https://oldcarbrochures.org/) and [1950 archive viewer](https://oldcarbrochures.org/December%202023/1950%20Chrysler%20Saratoga%20and%20New%20Yorker/slides/1950%20Chrysler%20Saratoga%20and%20New%20Yorker%20%28TP%29.pdf-2023-11-26%2012.2.19_Page_01.html).

## Chrysler.com exclusion

- FCA Website Terms of Use, last updated 2021-04-06, apply to Chrysler.com and prohibit automated systems from extracting site data.
- A Chrysler.com history page was fetched before these terms were reviewed. After discovering the restriction, its content was excluded from factual support; no page capture or claim from it was retained in the research files. The page is not used to support MY1963.
- Terms reviewed: [FCA Website Terms of Use](https://www.chrysler.com/crossbrand_us/terms-of-use).

## Savoy Automobile Museum

- The museum record supports a 1977 Chrysler New Yorker Brougham vehicle identity at the catalog's broader nameplate/year level.
- The FAQ says photographs cannot be published or commercially exploited. No photo was used. No separate grant for factual metadata reuse, automation, retention, derivation, or public display was found.
- See the [1977 museum record](https://savoymuseum.org/portfolio-items/1977-chrysler-new-yorker-4-door-sedan) and [museum FAQ](https://savoymuseum.org/faq).

## Provenance and disposition

- The imported Wikipedia claim rows do not identify an exact article revision, timestamp, revision-specific license, or complete fact locator. Their SHA-256 label is not a Wikimedia revision ID.
- MY1943–1945 is absent from this selected cohort; this audit does not independently establish the reason for that gap or assert a complete model-year availability history.
- The 1952, 1956, and 1977 facts have bounded identity support, but all 35 profiles remain on hold because imported-source provenance and source-use review are incomplete. The other 32 selected years remain factually unverified. No trims, generations, or configurations were assessed.

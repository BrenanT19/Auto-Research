# Model A independent-history search snippets

Search snippets retained as evidence only; not a substitute for full-page verification.

## Ford Motor Company, “The 1928 Ford Model A”
URL: https://corporate.ford.com/articles/history/the-1928-ford-model-a.html
Publication date: search result gave Mar. 23, 2021
Tier: 3 (manufacturer source; not independent)
Snippet: “Introduced to the public in December 1927, the 1928 Model A was an immediate sensation.”
Use: Supports Ford's own explicit distinction between December 1927 public introduction and the named 1928 car; this is not independent corroboration.

## Smithsonian Institution, “Ford Model A Automobile”
URL: https://www.si.edu/collections/snapshot/ford-model-automobile
Publication date: not shown in search snippet
Tier: 1 (national museum collection record)
Snippet: “Ford Model A Automobile, 1931, Smithsonian’s National Museum of American History. On Dec. 2, 1927, Henry Ford debuted the Ford Model A automobile, the successor to his Ford Model T.”
Use: Confirms one museum-held object is identified as 1931; by itself this does not establish full catalog coverage or final model year.

## Wikipedia, “Ford Model A (1927–1932)”
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Model_A_(1927%E2%80%9331)
Publication date: continuously edited; no pinned revision identified
Tier: 3 (tertiary, collaboratively edited)
Snippet: “Production | October 1927 – March 1932” and “Model years | 1928–1932”.
Use: Shows current search-indexed article text has 1928–1932 model years, which conflicts with sources describing the car's run as 1928–1931; no claim here about exact revision lineage.

## Hagerty Media, “Your handy 1928–31 Ford Model A buyer’s guide”
URL: https://www.hagerty.com/media/buying-and-selling/your-handy-1928-31-ford-model-a-buyers-guide
Publication date: not shown in search snippet
Tier: 2 (established automotive publisher; fetched evidence saved separately)
Snippet: “The 1930 Model A had a fully polished radiator shell, while 1931 sported reliefs with painted inserts. Even the headlights changed, from vertical fluted lenses in 1928 to Twolite branded lights in 1929…”
Use: Specifically distinguishes hardware by 1928/1929 and 1930/1931.

## This Day in Automotive History, “December 7, 1931 - The last Ford Model A”
URL: https://automotivehistory.org/december-7-1931-the-last-ford-model-a
Publication date: site snippet displayed Dec. 7, 2024
Tier: 3 (independent enthusiast history site; secondary)
Snippet: “The last Ford Model A left the assembly line on december 7, 1931.”
Use: Single-source production-end claim; contradicted by the Museum of American Speed and broader production chronology indicating production into March 1932, so do not use as evidence that 1931 was the final production date or model year without checking what “last” means.

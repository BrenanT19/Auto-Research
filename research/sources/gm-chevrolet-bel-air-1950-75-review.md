# Chevrolet Bel Air model-year source review: 1950–1975

- Reviewed: 2026-10-08.
- Publisher: General Motors, GM Heritage Archive.
- Scope: the 26 year-specific Chevrolet vehicle-information kits listed below.
- Method: inspect only extracted text needed to check whether “Bel Air” appears in a model, series, or body-style context for the kit's stated model year. The relevant text appeared in the first retrieved segment for MY1955–1959, MY1961, MY1963–1975, and in the next retrieved segment for MY1950–1954, MY1960, and MY1962. Only the relevant portions were checked; the full PDFs were not reviewed.
- Result: each kit independently supports Chevrolet / Bel Air / the stated model year. This does not establish a complete U.S. sales roster, every market's offering, or exact trim/body applicability.
- Provenance: the imported Wikipedia revision ID and timestamp remain unknown.
- Rights: GM's published notice prohibits reproducing or reposting its site materials without express written permission. Rights for factual-data retention or derivation and automated extraction remain unresolved. No source prose, tables, or images were copied or saved. All 26 profiles remain on hold.

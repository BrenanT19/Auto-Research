# Holley automotive-history source exclusion

- Reviewed: 2026-10-08.
- Terms source: [Holley Website & Application License Agreement](https://documents.holley.com/04162025_holley_-_terms_of_use_-_final.pdf), effective 2025-04-16.
- The agreement limits service use to personal, non-commercial purposes and prohibits automated copying or scraping without express written permission. Its stated search-engine exception is limited to indexing.
- The following truck-generation articles were considered as research leads and are excluded from vehicle-fact evidence because no written permission for automated collection is present:
  - [GM Truck Generations: 1960–1966](https://www.holley.com/blog/post/gm_truck_generations_1960-1966)
  - [GM Truck Generations: 1967–1972 Action Line](https://www.holley.com/blog/post/gm_truck_generations_the_1967-1972_action_line_series)
  - [GM Truck Generations: 1973–1987 Square Body](https://www.holley.com/blog/post/gm_truck_generations_1973-1987_square_body)
- No Holley article text, images, or factual claims are retained or used in the GMC C/K matrix. This record documents the exclusion and the applicable access condition only.

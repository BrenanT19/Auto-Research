# Car and Driver: Ford F-Series generation history

- **Publisher:** Car and Driver
- **Article date:** 2022-09-24 (search-result metadata)
- **Accessed:** 2026-10-08
- **Source tier:** Tier 2, independent automotive reporting
- **URL:** https://www.caranddriver.com/features/g23897696/ford-f-series-pickup-truck-history

The article's heading identifies the sixth generation as 1973–1979 and says the F-150 first appeared in 1975. This supports both ends of the F-150's 1975–1979 sixth-generation interval, when combined with Ford's own model-year launch history. It also reports that rectangular headlights were optional on upper trims in 1978 and standard in 1979, but this does not by itself establish the catalog's full 1978 front-end refresh. It provides no support for the 1976 split-grille facelift.

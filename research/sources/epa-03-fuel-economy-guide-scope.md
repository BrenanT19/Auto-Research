# 2026 Fuel Economy Guide — evidence note

- Source: https://www.fueleconomy.gov/feg/pdfs/guides/FEG2026.pdf?pubDate=20260402
- Title: 2026 Fuel Economy Guide
- Retrieved: 2026-10-08; guide identifies itself as updated September 29, 2026.
- Guide scope warning, paraphrased: certain high-GVWR vehicles are outside light-duty fuel-economy requirements, so fuel-economy estimates/labels need not exist; it also cautions that some information may arrive too late for inclusion.
- What this establishes: even the annual official consumer guide is not a complete census of all U.S. retail vehicle identities; exclusions can be regulatory/weight related or editorial-timing/data-availability related. The guide can strongly support listed records but omission does not support non-sale.

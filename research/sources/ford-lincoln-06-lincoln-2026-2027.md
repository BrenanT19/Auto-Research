## FAQs

### What SUV models does Lincoln make?

Lincoln makes four luxury SUVs for 2026: the [Lincoln Corsair® SUV](https://www.lincoln.com/luxury-suvs/corsair/2026/),\* the [Lincoln Nautilus® SUV](https://www.lincoln.com/luxury-suvs/nautilus/2026/), the [Lincoln Aviator® SUV](https://www.lincoln.com/luxury-suvs/aviator/2026/), and the [Lincoln Navigator® SUV](https://www.lincoln.com/luxury-suvs/navigator/2026/). The Lincoln Navigator is also offered as the extended-length Lincoln Navigator L SUV. Seating ranges from five passengers in the two-row models to up to eight in the full-size Lincoln Navigator.

### What are the Lincoln SUV models by size, smallest to largest?

From smallest to largest, the 2026 Lincoln SUV lineup ranks: Lincoln Corsair® SUV, Lincoln Nautilus® SUV, Lincoln Aviator® SUV, and Lincoln Navigator® SUV.

- Lincoln Corsair: 181.41 inches long, seats five
- Lincoln Nautilus: 193.2 inches long, seats five
- Lincoln Aviator: 199.7 inches long, seats up to seven
- Lincoln Navigator: 210 inches long, seats up to eight (Lincoln Navigator L SUV: 221.9 inches)

### Which Lincoln SUVs have three-row seating?

The 2026 Lincoln Aviator® SUV and 2026 Lincoln Navigator® and Navigator L SUV both offer three-row seating. The Lincoln Aviator seats up to seven passengers in a midsize footprint, while the Lincoln Navigator seats up to eight in a full-size body. The Lincoln Corsair® SUV and Lincoln Nautilus® SUV are two-row SUVs with seating for five.

### What is the towing capacity of Lincoln SUVs?

The 2026 Lincoln Navigator® SUV tops the lineup at up to 8,700 lbs when properly equipped.\*

- 2026 Lincoln Navigator and Navigator L: up to 8,700 lbs\*
- 2026 Lincoln Aviator®: up to 5,000 lbs\*
- 2026 Lincoln Corsair®: up to 3,000 lbs\* (with available tow package)
- 2026 Lincoln Nautilus®: up to 1,750 lbs\*

### Does Lincoln offer a hybrid SUV?

Yes. The 2026 Lincoln Nautilus® SUV offers an available hybrid on all trim levels, producing 285 combined horsepower\* and EPA-estimated 30 combined MPG.\*\* That’s more power and better fuel economy than the standard Nautilus 250 hp gas engine. Shoppers interested in a plug-in hybrid should explore [Lincoln Certified Pre-Owned inventory](https://www.lincoln.com/certified-used/).

### Which Lincoln SUVs offer hands-free highway driving?

Lincoln BlueCruise127 hands-free highway driving is available across all four 2026 Lincoln SUV models. BlueCruise helps control the braking and acceleration of your vehicle while keeping you centered in your lane on compatible divided highways while you keep your eyes on the road. BlueCruise equipped with four years included on the Lincoln Aviator® SUV, Lincoln Navigator® SUV, and Lincoln Nautilus® SUV, and is available on select Lincoln Corsair® SUV trims.

### Which Lincoln SUVs come with all-wheel drive?

The 2026 Lincoln Nautilus® SUV includes Intelligent AWD as standard on every trim. The 2026 Lincoln Aviator® SUV offer Intelligent AWD as an available option. The 2026 Lincoln Navigator® SUV uses a 4x4 system that’s available on Lincoln Navigator L SUV models and standard on standard-wheelbase models.

### How do Lincoln SUVs rate for safety?

Lincoln SUVs earn strong marks from both IIHS and NHTSA. The 2025 Lincoln Nautilus® SUV holds a 2025 IIHS _TOP SAFETY PICK+_, the institute’s highest honor.

- **Lincoln Nautilus®:** IIHS _TOP SAFETY PICK+_ (2025), IIHS _TOP SAFETY PICK_ (2026)
- **Lincoln Corsair®:** NHTSA 5-Star Crash Safety Ratings (2026 gas model only)\*
- **Lincoln Aviator®:** NHTSA 5-Star Crash Safety Ratings (2025)

### What’s the difference between the Lincoln Aviator® SUV and Lincoln Navigator® SUV?

The 2026 Lincoln Aviator® is a midsize three-row SUV seating up to seven. The 2026 Lincoln Navigator® SUV is a full-size three-row SUV seating up to eight.

- **2026 Lincoln Aviator**: 383 hp,\* up to 5,000 lbs towing,\*\* 18/25/20 MPG city/hwy/combined‡
- **2026 Lincoln Navigator**: 440 hp,\* up to 8,700 lbs towing,\*\* 48-inch panoramic display


Choose the [Lincoln Aviator® SUV](https://www.lincoln.com/luxury-suvs/aviator/2026/) for midsize agility or the [Lincoln Navigator® SUV](https://www.lincoln.com/luxury-suvs/navigator/2026/) for maximum space and capability. Check your local Lincoln Dealer for current pricing at lincoln.com.

### How does the Lincoln Nautilus® SUV compare to the Lincoln Aviator® SUV?

The 2026 Lincoln Nautilus® SUV is a two-row midsize SUV seating five. The 2026 Lincoln Aviator® SUV is a three-row midsize SUV seating up to seven.

- Lincoln Nautilus® SUV: 250 hp (2.0L turbo)\* or available hybrid (285 combined hp);\* EPA–estimated 30 combined MPG hybrid\*\*
- Lincoln Aviator® SUV: 383 hp/415 lb-ft,\* up to 5,000 lbs towing†


Choose the Lincoln Nautilus® SUV for two-row luxury with a hybrid option or the Lincoln Aviator® SUV for three-row family flexibility and more power. Check your [local Lincoln Dealer](https://www.lincoln.com/dealerships/) for current pricing at lincoln.com.

### What is Lincoln Black Label™?

Lincoln Black Label is the top-tier trim, adding curated interior themes, premium materials, and exclusive ownership perks. It’s available on the Lincoln Aviator® SUV, Lincoln Navigator® SUV and Navigator L SUV, and Lincoln Nautilus® SUV models. Available at participating [Lincoln Black Label Dealers](https://www.lincoln.com/dealerships/) only.

- Curated interior themes (e.g., Chalet, Flight, Yacht Club, Enlighten, Invitation, and Atmospheric)
- Four-year/50,000-mile premium maintenance plan\*
- Complimentary car washes and annual detail\*\*
- Lincoln Pickup & Delivery for service appointments†

24/7 lifetime Roadside Assistance (original owner)10

### Do 2026 Lincoln vehicles qualify for the federal auto loan interest tax deduction?

Yes. The 2026 Lincoln Corsair® SUV, Lincoln Aviator® SUV, and Lincoln Navigator® SUV may qualify for an annual deduction of up to $10,000 on auto loan interest under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act.\* Consult a tax professional to determine your specific eligibility.

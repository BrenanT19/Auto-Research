# Global Refresh for 2021+ Model S

Starting in 2022, some changes were introduced to Model S so
that it can be sold in international markets. To help differentiate from older versions,
parts that were introduced in this global refresh can be identified by the "Global" in the
procedure title, for example: "Headlight - LH **(Global)** (Remove and Replace)".

You will be redirected to your default email client to provide feedback.

Torque Calculator Values

T1 (Original torque spec)

L (Distance from center of wrench grip to center of square drive tang)

E (Horizontal distance from center of square drive tang to center of fastener)

Note

1. If the angle from the extension to the torque wrench is greater than 90° (Figure 2), input E as a positive number.
2. If the angle from the extension to the torque wrench is less than 90° (Figure 3), input E as a negative number.
3. If the angle from the extension to the torque wrench is exactly 90°, this calculator is not needed. Torque the fastener to T1.

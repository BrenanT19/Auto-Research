# Ford Thunderbird identity checks: MY1955–1977

- Reviewed: 2026-10-08.
- Scope: 23 selected historical Ford Thunderbird profiles, each with only make, model, and model year populated.
- Evidence checked: Car Model History's Thunderbird archive links an exact-year guide for every model year from 1955 through 1977. Each of those guides has a “Verified 2026-09-01 from” line that cites one or more period Ford documents or related records. The year-specific page and its cited source titles/links were checked; the underlying PDFs, brochures, and other records were not independently retrieved or reviewed.

Treat the batch as bounded secondary support, not a final manufacturer-backed verification.

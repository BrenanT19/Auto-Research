# Chrysler Newport brochure evidence review

- Reviewed: 2026-10-08.
- Scope: identity-level review of the 17 selected Chrysler Newport profiles for MY1961–1977. No production data, profile values, publication settings, or release settings were changed.
- Evidence is limited to the model name and model year. It does not establish a complete U.S. sales roster, exact body/trim/powertrain applicability, or a generation range.

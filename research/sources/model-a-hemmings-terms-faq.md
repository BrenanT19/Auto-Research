## Popular Questions

### How do I start an auction?

![Toggle](https://assets.hemmings.com/stories-dist/images/add-circle.svg)
Expand


First, submit your collector vehicle [here](https://www.hemmings.com/classifieds/bundles/carsforsale). Once we get it, we will review your vehicle and let you know if it's selected for the auction. Once selected, your vehicle is assigned to a real, live human [Listing Specialist](https://www.hemmings.com/stories/2019/07/11/hemmings-auctions-meet-the-team) — in Vermont, no less! Your listing specialist will reach out to you to learn more about your car and to share the process with you. They will craft your listing with a professional writer and schedule your auction start date. If your vehicle is not selected for the auction, the listing specialist will work with you to convert it to a Classified or Make Offer listing.

Read more about [how Hemmings Auctions work](https://www.hemmings.com/stories/2019/07/19/hemmings-auctions-how-it-works).

### How do I start a Make Offer listing?

![Toggle](https://assets.hemmings.com/stories-dist/images/add-circle.svg)
Expand


To generate a Make Offer listing and take the first step to selling your vehicle, go to [this page](https://www.hemmings.com/classifieds/bundles/carsforsale) and select Make Offer listing or become a member of the [Hemmings Motor Club](https://www.hemmings.com/membership/benefits) (members receive unlimited Make Offer listings for free!). Once you submit your vehicle information, we will review it and post online within 24 hours.

### How do I place a classified ad?

![Toggle](https://assets.hemmings.com/stories-dist/images/add-circle.svg)
Expand


Here is an overview of the process of placing a classified ad:

1. Select " [Sell a Car](https://www.hemmings.com/classifieds/bundles)" from the drop-down menu under "Buy & Sell"
2. Select the type of classified ad you would like (vehicle, engine & parts, etc.) from the options providedand click "Get Started Now!"
3. Click, "Start Your Listing" under the package you have chosen.
4. [Sign in or create an account](https://www.hemmings.com/account/register)
5. Be sure to have the following information available while filling out the form:
   - Vehicle location
   - Make
   - Model
   - Year
   - Mileage
   - Vehicle Condition
   - Vehicle Color
   - Price
   - Pictures for both the online and print portion of your ad.
   - Your contact information (phone number, email)
   - Payment method (credit card or PayPal).

### How do I clear my browser's cache if I'm having trouble with the website?

![Toggle](https://assets.hemmings.com/stories-dist/images/add-circle.svg)
Expand


**Clearing Cache on Computers**

1.     **Google Chrome**:

o   Click the three dots in the top-right corner.

o   Select **Settings** \> **Privacy and security** \> **Clear browsing data**.

o   Choose **All time**, check **Cached images and files**, and click **Clear data**

2.     **Microsoft Edge**:

o   Click the three dots in the top-right corner.

o   Go to **Settings** \> **Privacy, search, and services** \> **Clear browsing data**.

o   Select **Cookies and other site data** and **Cached images and files**, then click **Clear now**

3.     **Mozilla Firefox**:

o   Click the three lines in the top-right corner.

o   Select **Settings** \> **Privacy & Security** \> **Clear Data**.

o   Check **Cached Web Content** and click **Clear**

4.     **Safari (Mac)**:

o   Click **Safari** in the top menu bar.

o   Select **Settings** \> **Privacy** \> **Manage Website Data**.

o   Click **Remove All** and confirm

**Clearing Cache on Mobile Devices**

1.     **Google Chrome (iOS/Android)**:

o   Tap the three dots in the bottom-right corner.

o   Go to **Settings** \> **Privacy** \> **Clear browsing data**.

o   Select **Cached images and files** and tap **Clear data**

2.     **Safari (iOS)**:

o   Open the **Settings** app.

o   Scroll to **Safari** and tap **Clear History and Website Data**

**Quick Tip**

On most browsers, you can press **Ctrl + Shift + Delete** (Windows) or **Cmd + Shift + Delete** (Mac) to quickly open the cache-clearing menu.

### How can I find vehicles for sale?

![Toggle](https://assets.hemmings.com/stories-dist/images/add-circle.svg)
Expand


To search for Cars for Sale on Hemmings.com under Buy/Sell tab on homepage select: ["Cars for Sale"](https://www.hemmings.com/classifieds/cars-for-sale).

You can filter your search by make, model, year, price, and more or use the keyword search for free form typing.

Click on a listing to view the details.

### How does bidding work?

![Toggle](https://assets.hemmings.com/stories-dist/images/add-circle.svg)
Expand


We've made bidding as simple as possible, but it’s serious business, so don't make a bid without intent to buy the vehicle at that price. Once [registered](https://www.hemmings.com/auctions/account/register) and [logged in](https://www.hemmings.com/auctions/account/sign-in), you can bid on the listing page by clicking on the "Bid Now" button. For transparency, we show you your full cost, including Hemmings fees, before you confirm your bid.

Read more about how [Hemmings Auctions work](https://www.hemmings.com/stories/2019/07/19/hemmings-auctions-how-it-works).

### How do I get updates on a Make Offer listing or watch a Make Offer?

![Toggle](https://assets.hemmings.com/stories-dist/images/add-circle.svg)
Expand


When you find a Make Offer you want to keep tabs on, hit the "WATCH" button at the top of it and the magic will happen. You'll get update emails in your inbox until the vehicle is sold or expires.

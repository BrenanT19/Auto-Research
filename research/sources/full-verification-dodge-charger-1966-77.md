# Dodge Charger identity checks: MY1966–1977

- Reviewed: 2026-10-08.
- Scope: 12 selected historical Dodge Charger profiles for MY1966–1977. Each profile has only make, model, and model year populated.
- Evidence checked: Car Model History's Dodge Charger archive links an exact-year guide for every year in this batch. Each guide has a matching model-year heading and Dodge Charger description; the MY1966 guide expressly identifies the Charger as debuting for that model year. Treat these pages as candidate bounded secondary identity evidence only.
- Independence and source quality: The site disclaims accuracy and asks readers to verify important information independently. The MY1966 and MY1968 guides list the Dodge Charger Wikipedia article as their only named source; the MY1969 page lists Wikipedia articles for Charger and Charger Daytona. Other year pages list various brochures, dealership guides, factory specifications, and editorial references, but also cite Wikipedia in several cases. None of those underlying materials was independently retrieved or reviewed for this batch. A guide citation to the same Wikipedia article as the imported catalog claim is not independent corroboration.
- Claim boundary: The checked pages do not establish an exhaustive U.S. model-year roster, exact market availability, or trim/body/engine/package applicability. The broad Charger label is not an exact configuration claim.
- Source access and use: Car Model History's `robots.txt` permits crawlers, subject to law. Its Terms of Use disclaim accuracy, protect original text/layout/selection/arrangement, permit viewing and link sharing for personal non-commercial use, and require prior written permission for other use, including republication or commercial use. The robots policy permits crawling but is not a license for retaining or distributing derived facts. Reuse rights for the site's underlying references also remain unreviewed. No guide prose, images, tables, or underlying document contents were copied.
- Provenance: The original imported Wikipedia revision ID and timestamp remain unknown. The year guides do not recover that revision; several cite the same article and therefore do not resolve lineage.
- Disposition: All 12 profiles remain on hold pending independent source review, original import provenance, exact scope/applicability review, and source-use clearance.

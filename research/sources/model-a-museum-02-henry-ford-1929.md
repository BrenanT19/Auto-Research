THF88920 / 1929 Ford Model A Town Sedan

The 1929 model year was the second for production of Ford's Model A. The car received brighter trim and additional color options, as well as 12 new body styles. The Town Sedan was a deluxe version of the standard four-door Model A -- or "Fordor" in Ford's parlance. Total Model A production for the 1929 calendar year topped 1.5 million units.

### U.S. Ford Media and Press Contacts

To help the Ford Communications team respond quickly to reporters and editors, PR contact information is available exclusively to media members through the From the Road profile page. To access it, [register for Ford From the Road](https://www.fromtheroad.ford.com/us/en/registration) and select Media as your profile type. Once approved, log in, click your name in the upper-right corner, and view the PR Contact list in your profile.

For urgent inquiries or for login issues, email [fromtheroad@ford.com](mailto:fromtheroad@ford.com).

# GM Cadillac year-specific vehicle-information kit checks: MY1941–1974

- Reviewed: 2026-10-08.
- Publisher: General Motors, GM Heritage Archive.
- Scope: 42 selected historical Cadillac identity profiles across 22 year-specific Cadillac vehicle-information kits.
- Method: Check only the extracted model-name text needed for the matching year. The relevant name appeared in the first retrieved text segment for each of the 22 accessible kits; the complete PDFs were not reviewed.
- Result: Relevant text supports 13 Cadillac Series 62 profiles, 16 Eldorado profiles, and 13 DeVille profiles at make/model/year level. Series 62 evidence may use either the “Series 62” or “Sixty-Two” form. It does not establish a complete U.S. sales roster or exact variant, body, trim, or configuration applicability.
- Unavailable source text: The GM index lists general Cadillac kits for MY1946, 1948, 1950, 1958, 1963, 1964, and 1969, but text retrieval failed for those PDFs. No full-file downloads were attempted and no profile rows were inferred from those files. The index has no general Cadillac kit for Series 62 MY1940, MY1953, or MY1957, or for Eldorado/DeVille MY1975–1977. The separately indexed MY1953 Eldorado and MY1957 Eldorado Brougham entries remain the bounded index-title checks recorded elsewhere.
- Provenance: The imported Wikipedia revision ID and timestamp remain unknown.
- Rights: GM's published notice prohibits reproducing or reposting its site materials without express written permission. Rights for factual-data retention or derivation and automated extraction remain unresolved. No source prose, tables, or images were copied or saved.
- Disposition: All 42 profiles remain on hold. This check does not clear provenance or source-use conditions.

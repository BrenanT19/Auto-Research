What brands does General Motors own?

General Motors (GM) has a breadth of offerings that serve both consumers and businesses. GM’s family of vehicle brands — [Chevrolet](https://www.chevrolet.com/?evar25=gm_com_faq), [Buick](https://www.buick.com/?evar25=gm_com_faq), [GMC](https://www.gmc.com/?evar25=gm_com_faq), and [Cadillac](https://www.cadillac.com/?evar25=gm_com_faq) — ensures every driver can find the right vehicle. GM also offers a wide array of products to keep drivers on the road that span from automotive financing and software services to insurance and parts.

How do I know which vehicle is right for me?

We build vehicles for every kind of driver and journey, including: [electric,](https://www.gm.com/innovation/electric-vehicles) gas-powered, and vehicles with [advanced driver assistance systems](https://www.gm.com/innovation/av-safe-deployment), as well as trucks, SUVs, sedans, coupes, hatchbacks, and commercial models. Whether you’re seeking power, efficiency, space, or style, we’re here to help you find the one for you. Explore our online breakdowns or visit a [dealership](https://www.gmcertified.com/shopping-tools/locate-a-dealer?evar25=gm_com_faq) to find the one for every drive.

Where can I purchase a GM vehicle?

GM has over 4,000 dealerships across the country. Locate a [dealership](https://www.gmcertified.com/shopping-tools/locate-a-dealer?evar25=gm_com_faq) near you so that you can find the one for you.

Is General Motors Chevy?

[Chevrolet](https://www.chevrolet.com/?evar25=gm_com_faq) is one of four [GM brands](https://www.gm.com/our-brands). GM manufactures all Chevy vehicles.

Which GM brands have a racing team?

GM has a longstanding history with motorsports. Currently, GM backs two racing teams: [Chevrolet Motorsports](https://www.chevrolet.com/motorsports?evar25=gm_com_faq) and [Cadillac Racing](https://www.cadillac.com/performance?evar25=gm_com_faq). [The Cadillac Formula 1 team will join the 10 existing Formula 1 teams](https://news.gm.com/home.detail.html/Pages/news/us/en/2025/mar/0307-f1.html) starting in 2026 through its partnership with TWG Motorsports. Winning on the racetrack is in our DNA – GM’s [Chevy team](https://www.chevrolet.com/motorsports#:~:text=Thanks%20to%20an%20unforgettable%20effort,500%20wins%20than%20any%20manufacturer.) has the most wins of any racing team in NASCAR with an impressive 102 NASCAR National Series wins at Dayton.

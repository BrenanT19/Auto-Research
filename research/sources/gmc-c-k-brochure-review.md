# GMC C/K model-year source review: MY1960–1977

- Reviewed: 2026-10-08.
- Scope: all 18 selected GMC C/K profile rows and their populated make/model/year fields.
- Method: inspected extracted text from nine GMC brochures hosted by XR793. No brochure text, tables, or images were copied into this project. The seven supported years below have direct model-year markings or text; the 1962 and 1963 extracted text does not independently show its year.
- Result: period brochures provide bounded support for seven rows: MY1966, MY1967, and MY1973–1977. They support a broad GMC pickup/truck-family year, not a specific C/K retail badge, complete U.S. sales roster, or body/trim/configuration. Eleven rows remain unverified. All 18 remain on hold.
- Profile label: the catalog uses `GMC C/K` as a family-level label. The brochures use GMC light-truck, pickup, and series names; exact retail wording and the mapping from each period series to the umbrella label remain limited.
- Early-year conflict: the imported source is the Chevrolet C/K Wikipedia article, but its exact revision and timestamp are unknown. The Car Model History overview distinguishes C/K nomenclature beginning in MY1960 from a GMC-badged version beginning in MY1962. This is a secondary-source conflict lead, not a resolution: its accuracy and reuse conditions remain open, and no underlying primary record was independently identified for MY1960–1963. Do not add or remove profiles based on this lead alone.
- Other unresolved years: XR793's MY1962 and MY1963 brochure file labels identify candidate years, but the extracted brochure text does not. The archive also surfaced MY1964/1965 Suburban/panel material, which does not establish the selected light-duty GMC C/K profile. A MY1969 item is Canadian and is not U.S. evidence. The surfaced MY1970–1972 items did not establish the required U.S. light-duty GMC C/K identity.
- Source-use review: XR793's `robots.txt` permits crawling outside `/wp-admin/`; tested terms URLs returned no terms page. No license for the hosted scans or the original brochures was located. Rights for factual-data retention/derivation, automated retrieval, and public display remain unresolved; scan authenticity and chain of custody are also unverified. No source text, tables, or images were saved.
- The Holley truck-generation articles considered as research leads are excluded after review of Holley's Website & Application License Agreement; see `holley-automated-collection-exclusion.md`. No Holley article claims are used as evidence.
- Provenance: the exact Wikipedia revision ID, timestamp, revision-specific license, and article locator used for the imported facts remain unknown.

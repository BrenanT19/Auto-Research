Title: Chrysler Opens Orders for the Newly Refreshed 2027 Pacifica — Stellantis Media
Published: 2026-03-09
Fetched: 2026-10-08

Stellantis says orders opened for the 2027 Chrysler Pacifica and describes a refreshed exterior with a new front fascia, vertical LED headlamps, and illuminated grille. It says dealer arrivals were scheduled for summer 2026.

Supports: the 2027 model-year front-fascia refresh and continuation of the Pacifica minivan name.

Limits: a scheduled arrival is not evidence of a retail-sales count or proof of every exact build; this release does not identify the internal RU code.

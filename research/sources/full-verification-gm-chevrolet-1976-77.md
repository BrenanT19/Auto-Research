# Chevrolet Impala and C/K model-year source review: 1976–1977

- Reviewed: 2026-10-08.
- Publisher: General Motors, GM Heritage Archive.
- Scope: the 1976 and 1977 Chevrolet passenger-car kits and the matching Chevrolet Truck kits.
- Method: inspect only extracted model/series text relevant to Impala or C- and K-designated truck models. The complete PDFs were not reviewed.
- Result: each passenger-car kit lists Impala in model/series/body-style context; each truck kit contains C- and K-designated model references. This supports the stated make/model-year at family level, not a complete U.S. sales roster, exact retail wording for the combined C/K label, or exact body/trim applicability.
- Provenance: the imported Wikipedia revision ID and timestamp remain unknown.
- Rights: GM's published notice prohibits reproducing or reposting its site materials without express written permission. Rights for factual-data retention or derivation and automated extraction remain unresolved. No source prose, tables, or images were copied or saved. All four profiles remain on hold.

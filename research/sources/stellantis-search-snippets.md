## Source scope caveat

- NHTSA vPIC About: https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/About
  - Source date: not shown; fetched 2026-10-08.
  - Tier: 1, federal data-service documentation.
  - Fetched evidence: `research/sources/stellantis-05-vpic-scope.md`.
  - The page says vPIC is intended for MY1981 and forward and centers on VIN
    decoding/manufacturer-submitted identity data. It is not a historical
    consumer-sale chronology. Its own described scope makes it unsuitable as
    proof about pre-1981 retail availability or an absent vehicle.

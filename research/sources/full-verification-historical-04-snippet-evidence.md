## Model year versus calendar year

- **URL:** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-B/chapter-V/part-565
- **Title:** Part 565 — Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) Requirements
- **Publication date:** not surfaced in result
- **Exact search snippet:** “Model year means the year used to designate a discrete vehicle model, irrespective of the calendar year in which the vehicle was actually produced, Model Year”
- **Scope:** Federal VIN-rule definition distinguishing model year from production calendar year. It is a definition, not evidence for any particular historical vehicle identity.

## Revision history link in attribution

- **URL:** https://creativecommons.org/2009/03/09/wikipedia-and-attribution
- **Title:** Wikipedia and attribution
- **Publication date:** 2009-03-09
- **Exact search snippet:** “1) Link to the article must be given. 2) Collective credit (e.g. Wikipedia community). 3) Link to the version history must be given. 4) For online use: link. For other uses: full list of authors. 5) Full list of authors must always be copied. 6) No credit is needed.”
- **Scope:** A dated explanatory article about attribution guidance. Follow the applicable license and current Wikimedia guidance for the intended use; preserve an exact revision/history link and evaluate whether more detailed author attribution is required.

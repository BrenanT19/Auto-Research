# Chevrolet C/K model-year source review: 1960–1975

- Reviewed: 2026-10-08.
- Publisher: General Motors, GM Heritage Archive.
- Scope: the 16 year-specific Chevrolet Truck vehicle-information kits listed below.
- Method: inspect only retrieved model/series text for C- and K-designated Chevrolet truck models in the kit's stated year. The complete PDFs were not reviewed.
- Result: each year's kit contains C- and K-designated truck model references, supporting the broad combined C/K family and year. The profile's slash label is an umbrella shorthand; this evidence does not establish exact retail wording, body/trim availability, or configuration applicability.
- Provenance: the imported Wikipedia revision ID and timestamp remain unknown.
- Rights: GM's published notice prohibits reproducing or reposting its site materials without express written permission. Rights for factual-data retention or derivation and automated extraction remain unresolved. No source prose, tables, or images were copied or saved. All 16 profiles remain on hold.

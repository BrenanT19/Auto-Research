# Chevrolet Impala model-year source review: 1958–1975

- Reviewed: 2026-10-08.
- Publisher: General Motors, GM Heritage Archive.
- Scope: all 18 general Chevrolet vehicle-information kits for MY1958–1975, including years where the archive also has a distinct Impala kit title.
- Method: inspect only extracted text needed to check whether “Impala” appears in a model, series, or body-style context for the kit's stated model year. The relevant text appeared in the first retrieved text segment for all 18 kits; the complete PDFs were not reviewed.
- Result: each year-specific kit independently supports Chevrolet / Impala / the stated model year. This does not establish a complete U.S. sales roster, every market's offering, or exact trim/body applicability.
- Related review: the same MY1958–1975 general Chevrolet kits were also checked for Bel Air; see [the Bel Air review](gm-chevrolet-bel-air-1950-75-review.md).
- Provenance: the imported Wikipedia revision ID and timestamp remain unknown.
- Rights: GM's published notice prohibits reproducing or reposting its site materials without express written permission. Rights for factual-data retention or derivation and automated extraction remain unresolved. No source prose, tables, or images were copied or saved. All 18 profiles remain on hold.

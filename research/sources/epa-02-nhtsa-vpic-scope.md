# NHTSA vPIC About page — evidence note

- Source: https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/About
- Title: NHTSA Product Information Catalog and Vehicle Listing — About
- Retrieved: 2026-10-08; no publication date shown on the retrieved page.
- The agency describes vPIC as a system for VIN decoding, manufacturer information and related data, populated from manufacturer submissions/reports. It says the system is intended for MY1981-forward use and cannot decode vehicles predating the 1980s VIN standard. Public-use language is expressly associated with its open-data platform.
- What this establishes: scope and function of manufacturer-reported identification/VIN information. It is not an affirmative census of U.S. retail sales or a declaration that an unlisted nameplate/year was not sold. VIN decoding boundary is not the same as a universal model-list completeness threshold.
- Rights: NHTSA page states public use; retained here as paraphrased factual notes only.

## Notable Claims Requiring Cross-Reference

- Ram’s 7-grade list must not be copied wholesale onto Ram 3500: the manufacturer’s model-specific off-road announcement limits Power Wagon and Heavy Duty Rebel to 2500. Big Horn/Lone Star is one regional grade name, not two.
- The Ram matrix is a combined 2500/3500 table. Its grade/cab/bed availability is useful configuration evidence, but does not settle model-specific U.S. availability for every row; do not infer a complete buildable combination set from it.
- Lucid’s announcement’s four-name lineup is not itself a MY2024 availability table. Its range footnote discusses MY2023 Grand Touring/Touring/Pure AWD and MY2024 Pure RWD/Touring/Sapphire; the same release says Grand Touring availability was expected early 2024, and the EPA record verifies a MY2024 Grand Touring (“G Touring XR”) configuration. Thus the MY2023 footnote is not evidence that 2024 Grand Touring was absent. EPA trim-like naming is not a separate retail-trim taxonomy.

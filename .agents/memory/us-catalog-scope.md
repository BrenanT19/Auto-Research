The catalog target is **all U.S. model years** for Ford, Lincoln, Chevrolet,
Buick, GMC, Cadillac, Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, Ram, Tesla, Rivian, and Lucid.
Verified batches are allowed; a recent-years-only catalog is not a substitute.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected all U.S. model years and allowed verified
batches, without authorizing a silent reduction of historical coverage.

**How to apply:** Report each batch's exact additions, discovery retirements,
and remaining coverage. Do not treat existing illustrative demos as real
coverage. Establish year-specific retail trims rather than deriving them from
engines, batteries, wheels, drivetrain options, or current shopping pages.

Retire an illustrative profile from discovery only behind valid source-backed
identity gates. Preserve its records and relationships; do not transfer its
research or owner evidence to a real profile. Saved links may resolve to an
exact matching real retail trim, but an invalid demo trim must never be
redirected to a different real trim merely to avoid an unavailable page.

**Why:** The user requires safe retirement without breaking saved routes or
relationships. Illustrative ownership evidence is not evidence about a real
vehicle, even when its displayed brand/model/year/trim matches.

**How to apply:** Keep identity/rights/release gates independent; preserve
explicit archive behavior for invalid demos and fail closed when a replacement
loses eligibility. Existing design and sparse-profile constraints still apply.

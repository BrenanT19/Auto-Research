name: U.S. automotive catalog scope
description: All-history catalog scope and safe, source-backed retirement of illustrative profiles.
---

The catalog target is **all U.S. model years** for Ford, Lincoln, Chevrolet,
Buick, GMC, Cadillac, Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, Ram, Tesla, Rivian, and Lucid.
Verified batches are allowed; a recent-years-only catalog is not a substitute.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected all U.S. model years and allowed verified
batches, without authorizing a silent reduction of historical coverage.

**How to apply:** Report each batch's exact additions, discovery retirements,
and remaining coverage. Do not treat existing illustrative demos as real
coverage. Establish year-specific retail trims rather than deriving them from
engines, batteries, wheels, drivetrain options, or current shopping pages.

Each model/year must have its own discoverable vehicle card and dedicated
profile, using the established Mustang card styling and report layout.

**Why:** The user reiterated that every model year must be represented, not
just MY2024, and explicitly requested individual cards and profiles that look
like the Mustang presentation.

**How to apply:** Treat verified batches as incremental progress toward the
all-history catalog, never as fulfillment of the entire request. Preserve
year-specific identities and the established presentation.

Owner reviews, maintenance issues, and other detailed vehicle research are
future work requiring an explicit user command.

**Why:** The user wants these capabilities eventually but explicitly said not
to add or pull that information now.

**How to apply:** Keep catalog identity work separate from research ingestion.
Leave unresearched sections empty or labeled Not yet researched until the user
authorizes that research phase.

Historical calendar production dates are not interchangeable with model years.
Keep explicit wartime gaps, separate marques and U.S./Canadian end dates; do
not generate every integer year between a nameplate's first and last appearance.

**Why:** Reviewed classic sources distinguish calendar introductions from model
years, omit wartime models, and sometimes show longer Canadian-only runs. A
range-based import would turn these distinctions into false U.S. profiles.

**How to apply:** Require explicit model-year evidence for each retained range.
Where a source prints a different model name (such as Mustang II), preserve it
unless a separate verified family crosswalk justifies grouping it.

Retire an illustrative profile from discovery only behind valid source-backed
identity gates. Preserve its records and relationships; do not transfer its
research or owner evidence to a real profile. Saved links may resolve to an
exact matching real retail trim, but an invalid demo trim must never be
redirected to a different real trim merely to avoid an unavailable page.

**Why:** The user requires safe retirement without breaking saved routes or
relationships. Illustrative ownership evidence is not evidence about a real
vehicle, even when its displayed brand/model/year/trim matches.

**How to apply:** Keep identity/rights/release gates independent; preserve
explicit archive behavior for invalid demos and fail closed when a replacement
loses eligibility. Existing design and sparse-profile constraints still apply.

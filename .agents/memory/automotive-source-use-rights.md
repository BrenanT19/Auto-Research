---
name: Automotive source-use rights
description: Rights preflight for retaining and displaying normalized vehicle facts from external sources.
---

For automotive research, evaluate the actual source and its terms for access, commercial storage/caching, normalized facts, public display, attribution, and automation. Do not require Ford permission merely because facts concern a Ford vehicle, and do not treat Ford Brand Licensing as a general factual-specification gate. Distinguish independent factual fields from copyrighted prose, images, documents, and data presentation. Do not import those creative materials into AutoResearch.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected the sourcing approach: ordinary factual specifications do not require manufacturer permission simply because they concern that manufacturer's products. Source-specific contractual restrictions still need evaluation. Manufacturer involvement alone does not invalidate government-data reuse.

**How to apply:** Prefer authoritative structured/public sources. Where a source's intended-use rights are genuinely unclear, mark that source NEEDS RIGHTS VERIFICATION; do not block unrelated facts supported by another lawful source. Identify the specific source and use at issue rather than imposing a blanket brand-permission requirement.

Evaluate exact-configuration evidence and reuse rights independently. A verified configuration does not establish reuse permission, and unresolved reuse permission is not evidence that the configuration is invalid. Report each gate separately, including the missing evidence needed to resolve it.

Multiple independently usable sources may establish different identity attributes; do not require one document to prove every field. The cross-source mapping must still be defensible for the exact model year and retail trim/body, without contradiction or invented combinations. Current shopping pages cannot establish an older model year's availability merely because names and powertrains appear unchanged.

**Why:** The user explicitly authorized combined official factual evidence and minimal provenance, while rejecting copied creative material and carryover of an excluded source's terms to unrelated sources.

**How to apply:** Record what each source actually proves and the crosswalk between them. If research stops, identify the single missing factual relationship rather than repeatedly requiring a blanket manufacturer license.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires independent gate decisions for the first real-data pilot, rather than allowing one blocker to substitute for evaluating the other.

**How to apply:** Follow the latest explicit scope authorization: private-draft creation and public publication are separate approvals. Verify the exact market/year/trim/body/powertrain combination and material package predicates, using appropriately scoped lawful evidence. Do not invent combinations from independent option lists, generalize incomplete VIN decodes, or substitute snippets, other markets, or later years for the required configuration.

Treat direct EPA test-car data separately from FuelEconomy.gov/ORNL documents and label data. Test-car rows can corroborate joined powertrain attributes, but test results and rated horsepower are not automatically retail specifications or window-sticker MPG. They may lack retail trim/body distinctions.

**Why:** The pilot found direct EPA test-car rows with useful powertrain evidence but without the requested retail trim/body identity; their measurements must not be substituted for marketing output or label economy.

**How to apply:** Evaluate the direct EPA dataset's own reuse basis, retain exact row/test identifiers when used, and leave optional output/economy fields unknown unless their retail applicability is separately established.

Keep the first real-vehicle pilot narrowly scoped: do not redo completed architecture/migrations, start unrelated CI/recovery work, redesign the UI, or expand owner-submission functionality while resolving the pilot.

**Why:** The user repeated these exclusions to keep the effort focused on one private real-data draft.

**How to apply:** Run relevant existing canonical/provenance/applicability/sparse/demo-parity checks only when the draft reaches validation; do not treat background task updates as new implementation assignments.

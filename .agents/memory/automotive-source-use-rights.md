---
name: Automotive source-use rights
description: Rights preflight for retaining and displaying normalized vehicle facts from external sources.
---

For automotive research, public accessibility is not reuse permission. Verify the specific source's terms for commercial use, retention/caching, normalized or derived facts, public display/redistribution, attribution, and automated access before ingestion. A general government open-data statement may not resolve a particular jointly produced or third-party-fed dataset. Ford's current web terms require express written permission for commercial use of site materials; recheck the current terms and obtain permission where applicable.

**Why:** A source can publish useful facts or an API while its terms leave commercial storage, derived use, or redistribution unclear. This was true for FuelEconomy.gov's joint EPA/manufacturer data and Ford's product materials during the 2024 Mustang pilot preflight.

**How to apply:** Assess each proposed source and the actual intended product use independently. If scope is unclear, mark NEEDS RIGHTS VERIFICATION and do not ingest or publish dependent facts.

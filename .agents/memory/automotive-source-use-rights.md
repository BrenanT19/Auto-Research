---
name: Automotive source-use rights
description: Rights preflight for retaining and displaying normalized vehicle facts from external sources.
---

For automotive research, public accessibility is not reuse permission. Verify the specific source's terms for commercial use, retention/caching, normalized or derived facts, public display/redistribution, attribution, and automated access before ingestion. A general government open-data statement may not resolve a particular jointly produced or third-party-fed dataset. Ford's current web terms require express written permission for commercial use of site materials; recheck the current terms and obtain permission where applicable.

**Why:** A source can publish useful facts or an API while its terms leave commercial storage, derived use, or redistribution unclear. Site-document terms and structured-data licenses can have different scopes; inspect dataset-specific license metadata rather than assuming that manufacturer involvement alone prevents reuse.

**How to apply:** Assess each proposed source and the actual intended product use independently. If scope is unclear, mark NEEDS RIGHTS VERIFICATION and do not ingest or publish dependent facts.

Evaluate exact-configuration evidence and reuse rights independently. A verified configuration does not establish reuse permission, and unresolved reuse permission is not evidence that the configuration is invalid. Report each gate separately, including the missing evidence needed to resolve it.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires independent gate decisions for the first real-data pilot, rather than allowing one blocker to substitute for evaluating the other.

**How to apply:** The pilot is currently authorized for research only. Do not create a canonical record even if research resolves both gates without further authorization. Do not use government corroboration to invent a Ford configuration or substitute third-party mirrors, snippets, other markets, or later model years for Ford U.S. MY2024 evidence.

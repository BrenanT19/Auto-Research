name: Catalog generation and refresh scope
description: Approved scope and evidence limits for generation filtering across the U.S. catalog.
---

The user requested generation selection across every manufacturer and model, and explicitly chose generations **and major refreshes**, not a Tesla-only fix.

**Why:** Model S exposed a catalog-wide metadata gap. Correct shared filtering does not mean chronological coverage is complete.

**How to apply:** Label refreshes as refreshes, disclose incomplete model coverage, and do not present a small reviewed batch as completion of the full request. Never derive model-year boundaries solely from calendar launch dates.

Permit multiple candidate memberships for genuinely overlapping model years; do not use a broad browsing era to establish an exact factory build, trim or configuration.

**Why:** Acadia/Acadia Limited, Wrangler JK/JL and Ram DS/DT coexist within model years. Model S also has within-year updates that a model/year-only profile cannot distinguish.

**How to apply:** Preserve one vehicle identity and one search result while allowing multiple selectable era memberships. Keep configuration-specific research independently scoped.

Compare source model names case-insensitively for era membership, while preserving original source names in identity and provenance.

**Why:** Historical EPA labels such as `MUSTANG` and later `Mustang` share a browse model identity; exact display-name matching omitted historical years from researched groups.

**How to apply:** Audit all rows sharing the selected model key, not only rows with identical display text. Case normalization is not permission to merge distinct nameplates, body styles or markets.

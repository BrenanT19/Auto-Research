The official [FCA Website Terms of Use](https://www.chrysler.com/crossbrand_us/terms-of-use) states that its site-use license is personal and non-commercial. Section 4 prohibits robots, spiders, scrapers, and other automated means of extracting data from the covered Sites. This is separate from the copyright status of independent factual names and does not make those facts untrue.

**Why:** During catalog research on 2026-10-06, review of the official terms showed that automated extraction from Chrysler.com was not an appropriate way to source a new development batch. No Chrysler Pacifica identity was imported from that page.

**How to apply:** For Chrysler/FCA catalog work, use another authoritative source with compatible access terms or verify a lawful access basis before automated retrieval. Keep access restrictions distinct from copyright analysis of normalized facts; do not turn this source-specific restriction into a blanket permission requirement for all FCA facts.

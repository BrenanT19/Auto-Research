---
name: Automotive evidence types
description: Preserve the distinction between direct owner experiences and patterns researched from external sources.
---

Direct owner submissions and externally researched owner-reported issues are separate evidence types. Never present externally gathered patterns as experiences submitted by AutoResearch users, or combine the two under an ambiguous owner-report label.

**Why:** The user explicitly established this as a long-term architecture principle for the staged automotive research product. Mixing them would imply a source or firsthand experience that AutoResearch does not have.

**How to apply:** Preserve separate provenance and presentation when introducing real automotive data or owner submissions. Keep fictional layout samples visibly fictional, and reserve verified/source-backed labels for information with actual verification or sources.

Firsthand ownership is configuration-specific. A reviewed broader model, model-year, trim, or component scope is appropriate for a researched pattern, but does not establish firsthand ownership of every vehicle underneath it. Forum research is external evidence, not an AutoResearch user submission. Do not promote old illustrative reports by changing a demo flag or matching visible labels.

**Why:** Vehicle labels can coincide across demo and canonical identities, and externally collected stories do not establish a direct submission to this product.

**How to apply:** Require an exact canonical configuration and reviewed applicability for direct experiences. Use the separately classified, documented scope for external patterns; keep original demo links and sample labels intact. Mixed community lists must label individual records rather than call every report fictional.
